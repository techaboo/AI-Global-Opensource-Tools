# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or technical choices.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Welcome corrections and differing levels of experience.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Respect privacy and do not publish personal information or secrets.
- Accept moderation decisions and repair harm where possible.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, impersonation, deliberate misinformation, retaliation, and publishing another person's private information without permission are unacceptable. Promotional manipulation of catalog fields, fabricated evidence, and undisclosed coordinated influence also undermine this community.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents the project in public or private spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not report sensitive conduct incidents in a public issue. Contact the repository owner privately through the contact options on their GitHub profile. Include relevant links or screenshots, dates, context, requested safeguards, and any immediate safety concern. Share only information you have a right to provide.

The owner will review reports as promptly and impartially as practical, protect confidentiality to the extent possible, and may seek additional context. Possible responses include a clarification, warning, content removal, temporary participation restriction, permanent ban, or referral to a platform or appropriate authority. Knowingly false reports or retaliation may themselves violate this policy; an unsubstantiated good-faith report does not.

If the owner is the subject of a report, use GitHub's platform reporting and blocking mechanisms. The project currently has no independently documented enforcement committee and should not imply otherwise.
